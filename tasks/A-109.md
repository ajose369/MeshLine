# A-109 — Do not lose group messages in flight across an epoch rotation

**Lane:** Claude (Lane A) · **Milestone:** M1 — Release blockers · **Needs:** A-103

## Done when
- [ ] ADR first: how long a previous epoch's key is kept, and what that costs if a device is seized
- [ ] Test: Alice adds Carol while Bob has not got the update; Alice can read what Bob sent under the old epoch
- [ ] Test: a removed member's messages under the old epoch are still refused
- [ ] The old key is receive-only and bounded, and old state still opens

## Context
- Split from A-103 (limit recorded in `docs/decisions/2026-10-31-group-membership-epochs.md`). A rotation deletes the old
  key, so a member who has not got the update sends messages nobody on the new epoch can read. Rotating on every add
  makes this more frequent, and on a delay-tolerant mesh the gap can last hours.
- The trade-off to settle: a retained key lets a seized device read recorded traffic of that epoch for longer. Consider
  dropping it at once after a removal, or when every member has acknowledged the new epoch.
