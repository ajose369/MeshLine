### Two-device on-device verification steps for user (unverified on real devices):
1. Install `./gradlew assembleDebug` on Phone A and Phone B.
2. **Queued state:** With Phone B's Bluetooth turned off, send a chat message from Phone A to Phone B. Observe that Phone A immediately displays "waiting for a peer" (`QUEUED`), NOT "relayed" or "delivered".
3. **Sent / Relayed state:** Turn on Phone B's Bluetooth. When Phone A connects and finishes transmitting all GATT fragments of the message packet to Phone B, observe Phone A's message status transitions to "relayed" (`SENT`).
4. **Delivered state:** When Phone B receives and processes the packet, it signs and returns an authentic `Ack` packet citing `acked_message_id`. Upon receiving the ACK, Phone A's status must transition to "delivered" (`DELIVERED`) with a green indicator.
5. **BLE retry on failed last fragment (B-101):** Send a multi-frame packet (large chat or SOS) from Phone A while moving Phone B to the fringe of BLE range or inducing a disconnection on the final fragment write. Observe that Phone A does not mark the packet as sent; instead, the uncompleted whole packet is requeued and successfully retransmitted upon reconnection.
